# Groups & channels

Understand community spaces, member roles and the controls available to you.

## 1. Choose the right space

Groups support conversations between members. Channels offer a way to publish updates to an audience. Explore the options shown in the app to choose the space that suits your community.

## 2. Find or join a community

Use the available discovery tools, public links or invitations to reach the intended group or channel. Check its name, description and administrators before sharing information. Some communities require an invitation or approval to join.

## 3. Understand roles and permissions

Owners, administrators and members can have different capabilities. Posting, inviting people, managing messages and changing settings depend on the permissions granted in that community. A control may be unavailable if your current role does not permit it.

## 4. Share and interact

Read the community’s description and pinned information before posting. Use replies, comments or reactions where they are available. In a channel, the options for audience participation depend on its configuration.

## 5. Manage a community

If you have the required permissions, use the community management controls to review its details, invitations, members and available moderation settings. Check the effect of an action before applying it to other members or published content.

## 6. Report an issue

Use Report on the relevant content or account where available. Include useful context so a reviewer can locate the issue. If you cannot access the app or complete a report, contact support@veepick.com. Do not send sensitive account credentials.

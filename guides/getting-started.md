# Getting started

Your first steps with Veepick, from installing the app to finding your way around.

## 1. Install Veepick

Visit the official Android download page and follow the instructions for your device. If Android asks you to allow an installation, review the source and the system prompt before continuing. Download Veepick only from an official destination linked by Veepick.

## 2. Create or access your account

Open the app and follow the on-screen registration or sign-in steps. Complete any verification requested in the app. Keep your password and sign-in codes to yourself, including when contacting support.

## 3. Set up your profile

Choose your profile name, username and photo using the available profile controls. A recognizable profile makes it easier for people to find the right account. Review what you share before making it visible to others.

## 4. Explore the app

Discover posts and videos, open your conversations, and explore groups or channels. The layout and available options can vary by app version and account. Use the labels and controls shown in your installed version.

## 5. Make it work for you

Review notification, privacy and account settings in the app. Device permissions can be managed in your Android settings. Features such as recording or calling may require the relevant camera or microphone permission.

## 6. If something does not work

Note what you were trying to do, the steps leading to the issue and your app version. Contact support@veepick.com with a short description. A screenshot can help after you hide private information. Never include passwords or verification codes.

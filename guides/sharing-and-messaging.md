# Sharing & messaging

A practical introduction to posts, videos, stories and everyday conversations.

## 1. Share a post or video

Use the creation controls in the app to select or record content. Review your media, text and available audience options before publishing. Keep the app connected while an upload is in progress and wait for the final publishing confirmation.

## 2. Understand upload progress

An upload indicator reaching 100% may mean the transfer has finished while publishing or media processing is still completing. If the post does not appear or an error is shown, check the result before starting a duplicate upload. Share the error message with support if the issue continues.

## 3. Use stories and comments

Stories offer another way to share updates, while comments keep a conversation connected to a post. Check the controls available on the relevant content for replies, reactions and management options. Only share media you have permission to use.

## 4. Start a conversation

Open an existing conversation or use the app’s available controls to find someone and start a chat. Review the recipient before sending text, media or files. Delivery, read and connection indicators reflect the information currently available to the app.

## 5. Calls and device permissions

Use the call controls available in a supported conversation or community. Android may ask for microphone or camera access when needed. If audio or video is unavailable, check the relevant permission, your connection and the app’s current call state.

## 6. Manage your experience

Use the available notification, blocking, reporting and conversation settings to manage interactions. Available actions depend on the content, your role and the app version. For help with a specific message or upload, include the time and a clear description without exposing private conversations.

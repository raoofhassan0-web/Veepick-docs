A LITTLE HELP GOES A LONG WAY

Questions?
You’re in the right place.

Find a quick answer, or get help with your app or account.

Contact support
Where can I download Veepick?

Use the official Android download page for the available download and installation information.

What should I read first?

Start with Getting started. It covers installation, your account, your profile and finding your way around.

Why do some options look different in my app?

Features and labels may vary by app version, device, account and community permissions. Follow the controls shown in your installed version.

How do I report an app problem?

Use in-app support or email support@veepick.com. Include your app version, device, what happened and the steps leading to it. Hide private information in screenshots.

Can I suggest a change to these guides?

Yes. Open a documentation issue on GitHub for an unclear explanation, a typo or a missing guide. Send account-specific requests directly to support.

Does this repository include the app source code?

This repository publishes Veepick’s public documentation and website. The application source repository is private.
